To report a Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) case anonymously in SSNeP+, you can follow these steps:

  1. Anonymous Reporting Mechanism: SSNeP+ have an anonymous reporting mechanism in place, such as a dedicated hotline, email address, or online form specifically for reporting PSEA cases.

  2. Contact Information: Obtain the contact information for the anonymous reporting mechanism from SSNeP+ staff, posters, or the organization's website.

  3. Report Details: Prepare all relevant details of the PSEA case you wish to report, including date, time, location, individuals involved, and any supporting evidence or information.

  4. Submit Report: Use the designated anonymous reporting channel to submit your report of the PSEA case. Provide as much detail as possible while maintaining your anonymity.

  5. Follow-Up: After submitting the report, you may receive a confirmation or reference number. SSNeP+ have procedures to refer victims cases to UNFPA for further investigation.

  6. Protection of Reporter: SSNeP+ should refer victims for safeguards in Safe house supported by partners while waiting investigation process.

  7. Monitoring and Feedback: SSNeP+ should establish mechanisms to monitor the progress of the reported case and provide feedback on the actions taken in response to the report.

By following these steps, you can report a PSEA case anonymously in SSNeP+ while ensuring that your identity is protected and the reported case is appropriately addressed.
