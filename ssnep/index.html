WE ARE ON MISSION CHANGING LIVES FOR GOOD

The South Sudan Network of People Living with HIV (SSNeP+) isn't just another organization. We are on a mission, a relentless pursuit to transform the lives of individuals living with HIV/AIDS (PLHIV) in South Sudan.

SSNeP+ is fully committed to its vision of creating an environment in which PLHIV live longer, positively and productively. SSNeP+ subscribes to the three (3) ‘zeros’ of the Joint United Nations Joint Programme on HIV/AIDS (UNAIDS), namely; Zero New Infections, Zero AIDS-related Deaths and Zero Discrimination.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONSGOT QUESTIONS?

SSNeP+ has anonymous reporting channels through dedicated hotlines and email. You can submit details of any PSEA case maintaining full anonymity. Victims are safeguarded in partner safe houses during investigations.

SSNeP+ explicitly promotes equal healthcare opportunity for women, men, girls, and boys. HR guidelines enforce strict accountability for gender sensitive issues in strategic planning and programs.

SSNeP+ enforces child safeguarding core principles across all projects. Mandatory risk assessments ensure children are protected during all organizational outreach activities.

SSNeP+ stands for South Sudan Network of People Living with HIV. It is an umbrella network representing state networks, support groups, and member associations across South Sudan.

SSNeP+ provides voluntary HIV testing & counseling, ART linkage, malaria net distribution, TB screening, capacity building workshops, and anti-stigma community advocacy.
