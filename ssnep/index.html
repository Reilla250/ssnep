We Work to Improve Lives
of PLHIV in South Sudan

SSNeP+ is a national umbrella organization that brings together various state networks, associations, and support groups of PLHIV across South Sudan, providing a unified voice for PLHIV.

Read More Contact Us

We Are On Mission

Changing Lives
for Good

SSNeP+ coordinates the interests and activities of member associations and support groups of People Living with HIV/AIDS throughout South Sudan, working towards Zero New Infections, Zero AIDS-related Deaths, and Zero Discrimination.

Malaria Programs

Malaria is a serious threat in South Sudan, but SSNEP+ is working hard to change that. We focus on prevention, quick diagnosis, and proper treatment.
