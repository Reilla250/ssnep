About Us

SOUTH SUDAN NETWORK OF PEOPLE LIVING WITH HIV

Coordination • Advocacy • Empowerment • Peer Support

South Sudan Network of People Living with HIV (SSNeP+) is a national umbrella body representing and coordinating the interests and activities of PLHIV member associations and groups across South Sudan. Registered as a non-governmental organization under the Ministry of Legal Affairs and the Relief and Rehabilitation Commission (RRC), SSNeP+ brings together state networks, community support groups, and healthcare advocates to ensure zero discrimination and equitable access to care.

SSNeP+ serves as a Sub-Sub Recipient (SSR) of Global Fund grants and maintains partnership agreements with UNFPA, Cordaid, UNDP, and USAID/AHEC. Through strategic community interventions, SSNeP+ advocates for the rights of PLHIV, distributes essential medical and nutritional supplies, and strengthens health service delivery across the 10 states of South Sudan.

Zero New Infections

Expanding access to targeted community-based testing, peer-led education, and preventive counseling for key and vulnerable populations.

Zero AIDS-Related Deaths

Ensuring 100% linkage to ART treatment, TB co-infection management, viral load tracking, and nutritional support for PLHIV households.

Zero Discrimination

Combating stigma in healthcare facilities, promoting gender equality, human rights, and enforcing child protection safeguards nationwide.
