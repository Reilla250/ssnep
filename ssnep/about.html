About Us

South Sudan Network of People Living with HIV (SSNeP+) is a national umbrella body that represents and coordinates the interests and activities of member associations and groups. SSNeP+ is an umbrella of State networks, member associations and support groups of PLHIV in South Sudan. SSNeP+ is registered in South Sudan with Ministry of Legal Affairs, South Sudan Relief and Rehabilitation Commission (RRC) and National NGO Forum (NNGO) Forum as non-governmental organisation (NGO).

SSNeP+ is fully committed to its vision of creating an environment in which PLHIV live longer, positively and productively. SSNeP+ subscribes to the three (3) 'zeros' of the Joint United Nations Joint Programme on HIV/AIDS (UNAIDS), namely; 'Zero New Infections', 'Zero AIDS-related Deaths' and 'Zero Discrimination', and ultimately works towards the attainment of these three (3) 'zeros' in South Sudan.

🚫

Zero New Infections

Working tirelessly to prevent new HIV infections across South Sudan through education, testing, and community outreach programs.

💊

Zero AIDS-related Deaths

Ensuring all PLHIV have access to life-saving antiretroviral treatment and comprehensive care services throughout the country.

🤝

Zero Discrimination

Eliminating stigma and discrimination against PLHIV in healthcare facilities, workplaces, and communities across South Sudan.
